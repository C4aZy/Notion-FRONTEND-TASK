Frequently Asked Questions

What is Notion?

Notion is an all-in-one workspace for notes, tasks, databases, and team collaboration — think of it as your personal and professional operating system.

Who can attend the workshop?

The workshop is open to all currently enrolled students of VIT Bhopal. Faculty and staff may attend subject to seat availability.

Is there a registration fee?

No, the workshop is completely free of cost for all registered participants.

Do I need prior experience with Notion?

No prior experience is required. The workshop is designed to take you from zero to proficient, covering everything from the basics to advanced use cases.

What should I bring?

Bring your own laptop with a modern browser installed. Make sure to create a free Notion account at notion.so before the event. A charger is also recommended for the full-day session.

Will there be a certificate of participation?

Yes, all participants who attend the full session will receive a digital certificate of participation issued by VIT Bhopal Notion Club.

What happens if I miss a session?

Attendance is mandatory for the entire duration. However, session recordings may be shared with registered participants after the event at the organizer's discretion.

How do I contact the organizers?

Reach out to the Workshop Coordinator (ABC) or Event Manager (PQR) via the official VIT Bhopal Notion Club email or their campus contact channels.
